Who I am

I'm NOCT, a Nepal-based video editor and visual artist working primarily in Adobe After Effects. My work sits at the intersection of music, rhythm, atmosphere, and cinematic storytelling — visuals that feel connected to the sound rather than simply placed on top of it.

Whether it's a music video edit, a visualizer, a cinematic sequence, or an artist promo, I focus on mood, pacing, typography, and visual detail to create something that feels immersive and memorable.

I'm especially interested in working with musicians, rappers, singers, producers, and independent artists who want thoughtful visuals that support their music and identity. If that's you, let's talk.
